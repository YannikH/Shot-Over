# Ammunition and Logistics

The gunline is stocked from containers, through pallets and crates, down to the single shells the loaders carry to the gun.

## Shells

| Shell | Type |
| --- | --- |
| **M1 105mm HE Shell** | High explosive |
| **M1 105mm AB Shell** | High explosive, airburst |
| **M546 105mm FRAG Shell** | Fragmentation |
| **M60 105mm WP Shell** | White phosphorus |
| **M60 105mm Smoke Shell** | Smoke |
| **M314 105mm LUME Shell** | Illumination |
| **M444 105mm ICM Shell** | Cluster munition (ICM) |

A shell is carried with ACE's carry action. Its charge is set on the shell itself (interaction on the shell → **Set Charge**); the charge is kept when the shell is put down or handed over. Loading is described on [The M101 Howitzer](gun.md#firing-a-round).

## Crates

A crate holds **2 shells** of one type. **Unpack Crate** opens it and the two shells lie in it, ready to be carried out. A crate can be carried, dragged and loaded as ACE cargo.

## Pallets

A pallet stacks crates of one type:

| Pallet | Crates | Rounds | ACE cargo size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **x42 Pallet** | 21 | 42 | 5 |
| **x24 Pallet** | 12 | 24 | 5 |
| **x18 Pallet** | 9 | 18 | 5 |

**Take** on a pallet takes one crate: it is put on the ground in front of you, to carry with ACE. The action shows how many crates are left. A pallet can be dragged, carried and loaded as ACE cargo.

## Cargo containers

A **x 504 Cargo Container** holds **252 crates** (504 rounds) of one type. It shows no crates; the **Take** action says how many are left.

- **Take** (crate): a crate, put on the ground in front of you.
- **Take** (pallet): a pallet next to you, as full as the container's stock allows: an x42 pallet while it has 21 crates left, then smaller ones.

There are containers for HE, AB, FRAG, WP, Smoke and LUME.
