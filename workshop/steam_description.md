# Shot, Over!

**Shot, Over!** makes crewing the M101 howitzer of S.O.G. Prairie Fire a gunline job: carry the shells, set the charge, load the breech by hand, and work out the firing data in the FDC notebook.

Created by YannikH and toadball of the original ITC team (with Yax); this version continues their unreleased mod, as YannikH invited.

# Requirements

- [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3)
- [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3)
- [S.O.G. Prairie Fire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1227700/) (CDLC)

# Features

- **M101 loaded by hand** — open the breech, carry a shell with its charge to it, close and fire
- **Shells, crates and pallets** to stock the gunline
- **FDC notebook** — charge, elevation and time of flight from grid and elevation
